CSV Cleaner

Your friendly tool to remove duplicate rows from CSV files!

How it Works:

  1. Upload your messy CSV file below.
  2. **Select ONLY the column(s) you want to use to find duplicates.**
  3. Click 'Clean My Data' and get your fresh file!

⚠️ **Important:** This tool only removes duplicates based on your selection. It's not a magical fix-all for other data issues.
